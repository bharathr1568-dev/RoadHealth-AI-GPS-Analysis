About RoadCare AI Platform

An end-to-end civic technology platform bridging citizens and municipal engineers for rapid road maintenance.

YOLO11m-P2P3-CBAM Attention

Our champion research architecture augments YOLO11 with high-resolution P2→P3 multi-scale detail feature extraction and Convolutional Block Attention Modules (CBAM) to reliably detect fine cracks and deep potholes.
